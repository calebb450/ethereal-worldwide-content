02 · What made it work

Five things
to repeat

1

Show the whole range at once

Every colour side by side, flat, from above. Someone sees the product, the colours and the quality in one second. The two lineup photos made 8 of your 9 orders. The single-item photos and the zip-up photos made none. On Instagram, the flatlay carousel got 25 shares, more than most reels.

2

Shorts open the door

8 of 9 orders came from shorts ads. At $100 they're the easiest yes. Lead every ad with them and let the store sell the set and the zip-up once people are there. People barely clicked the zip-up photos, so Meta stopped showing them.

3

Real people, real places

Your three biggest Instagram posts are all candid, full-body, phone-shot: a squat in a parking garage (1,412 reached), the Washed Black fit in a garage (1,041), a night street shot (1,005). No studio. You see the whole length of the shorts and how the set hangs.

4

Hype gets shared

"Samples are here, drop date coming soon" is your most shared post ever (58 shares). The lifestyle teaser got 39 and the white-wall reel got 40. Plain "available now" posts sit around 250–300 reach and almost no shares. Build anticipation before every drop.

5

Say the price and the speed

The ads that sold said the price, free shipping and how fast it ships, right at the top. "Ships next day" did better than "made to order." The set video that sold ($300) opened with "$260 if you buy the pieces apart. $150 for the set." Give people a number to react to.

05 · Shoot this next

The shot list

All on your phone, in natural light, no filters. Shoot photos at 4:5 for the feed and video at 9:16 for reels and stories.

1The lineup, againAll three shorts flat on concrete, shot straight down in daylight. Then do it on asphalt, a wood floor and a car hood. Same layout each time. This is your best seller, so make five versions of it.
2The full lineupBoth zip-ups above the three shorts, same floor, same angle. It shows the whole capsule in one frame.
3Three friends, three coloursOne person in each colourway, standing together, full body, in a parking garage. It's the lineup photo with real people in it.
4Garage fit check8–12 seconds, full body, phone in hand, a slow turn. Your top post was exactly this. Show the whole length of the shorts.
5Night walkThe set on a street at night, under shop lights, walking toward the camera. Your third-biggest post.
6The fabric5 seconds up close: pull the 22oz terry, show the wash fade and the raw hem. It shows people why it costs what it does.
7The teaserA week before anything new: "samples are here," with the pieces unboxed or on a rack. Don't show everything yet. Your most shared post was this.
8The captionPut the price, free shipping and "ships next day" in the first line. Leave the vibe line for the end.
