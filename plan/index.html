Ethereal Worldwide · Instagram blueprint · Nov 17 – Dec 31

230 → 3,300+

How to grow the page and build a real community around Ethereal in six weeks. Do these every day and every week, and you'll start 2027 on track for 5k.

Every day

The daily rules

  • 1One reel a day, 5–7pm LA time7–12 seconds, filmed on your phone, a trending sound at low volume. Missing a day costs more than a weak post.
  • 2Three stories a dayYour day, a fit in the mirror, a poll ("blue or black?"). Stories are where people get to know you.
  • 3Answer every comment in the first hourReply with a question so they comment again. Instagram shows a post to more people when it gets comments early.
  • 4Reply to every DMEvery single one, even just "appreciate you." The people you talk to are the ones who stay.
  • 515 minutes of commenting on other pagesReal comments (not emojis) on 20 posts from LA streetwear and fit-check pages. Their followers click your name.
What to post

Six reels, on repeat

Rotate these. Your two most-seen posts were full-body fits in a garage and on the street, so those come up most often.

1 · Garage / street fit check

Full body, slow walk toward the camera or a slow turn. Show the whole fit, head to shoes. 3× a week.

On screen: "Fit check. Garage edition."

2 · Rate the fit

One fit, then ask for a score. Easy to answer, so people comment, and comments push the reel to more people.

On screen: "Rate this 1–10. Be honest."

3 · The 22oz test

Put a normal hoodie next to yours. Hold both up, drop both, pull the fabric. People share this kind of side-by-side.

On screen: "Your hoodie vs 22oz French terry."

4 · Behind the brand

New samples arriving, packing orders, you talking to the camera for 10 seconds about why you started. Your "samples are here" post was your most shared post ever. People follow a person, not a logo.

On screen: "Building Ethereal, day 1."

5 · You pick

Let them choose: the next colour, which shorts, which photo goes on the next drop. Show the result a week later so they see you listened.

On screen: "You're picking the next colour."

6 · Gifting a stranger

Walk up to someone in LA with a good fit, ask about their outfit, give them a piece. Film their reaction. Once a week. These get shared well beyond your followers.

On screen: "Gifting strangers with good fits."
Growth

Where the 3,000 come from

Daily posting keeps the page alive. These bring in people who have never seen Ethereal.

About 1,200

Collab posts

Gift a piece to 6 LA creators (5k–50k followers, real fits, no meme pages). Each one posts a reel as an Instagram Collab with you, so it shows on both pages and their followers see your name. Aim for 2 a week.

About 1,300

Two giveaways

Nov 24–30 and Dec 15–21, one set each. To enter: follow @ethereaaaa.l, tag 2 friends, share it to your story. Co-host each one with one of your collab creators so their followers enter too. Pick the winner live on stories.

About 500

Showing up everywhere

The daily reels, the 15 minutes of commenting, and replying to comments with a reel. Every one of these puts your name in front of someone new.

Community

Make them part of it

Followers are a number. A community buys every drop, shows up and brings friends.

  • 1Give them a nameSomething from "Be light in all things." Use it in every caption and story so people feel they're in on it.
  • 2Repost every fitAnyone who tags you wearing Ethereal goes on your story. Save them all to a highlight called FAM. People post to get featured.
  • 3Close Friends listEveryone who has bought from you or comments often. They get first look at new pieces and behind-the-scenes before anyone else.
  • 4Let them decideA poll every week: the next colour, the next piece, the next shoot spot. Then show what they picked.
  • 5One LA link-up in DecemberMeet at a spot in LA, shoot fits together, hang out. Everyone posts it, and that's a week of content.
