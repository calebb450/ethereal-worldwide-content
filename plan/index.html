Six weeks, one reel a day, three ways to reach people who don't follow you yet. This plan adds 3,000 followers by New Year's and puts you on track for 5k.

Every day

The daily rules

  • 1One reel a day, 5–7pm LA time7–12 seconds, filmed on your phone, a trending sound at low volume. Missing a day costs more than a weak post.
  • 2Three stories a dayPacking an order, a fit in the mirror, a poll ("blue or black?"). Stories keep the people you just gained.
  • 3Answer every comment in the first hourReply with a question so they comment again. Instagram shows a post to more people when it gets comments early.

What to post

Six reels, on repeat

Rotate these. Your two most-seen posts were full-body fits in a garage and on the street, so those come up most often.

1 · Garage / street fit check

Full body, slow walk toward the camera or a slow turn. Show the whole length of the shorts. 3× a week.

On screen: "22oz set. $150. LA."

2 · Comment your size

Try the set on, then point at the screen. Everyone who comments gets the link in their DMs. Comments push the reel to more people.

On screen: "Comment SET + your size, I'll send it."

3 · The 22oz test

Put a normal hoodie next to yours. Hold both up, drop both, pull the fabric. People share this kind of side-by-side.

On screen: "Your hoodie vs 22oz French terry."

4 · Behind the brand

Packing orders, a new batch arriving, you talking to the camera for 10 seconds about why you started. Your "samples are here" post was your most shared post ever.

On screen: "Packing your orders. LA."

5 · 1, 2 or 3?

All three shorts, or both sets, side by side on concrete. Ask which one. Easy to answer, so people comment.

On screen: "Pick one. Wrong answers only."

6 · Gifting a stranger

Walk up to someone in LA with a good fit, ask about their outfit, give them a set. Film their reaction. Once a week. These get shared well beyond your followers.

On screen: "Giving strangers a 22oz set."
Where the 3,000 come from

Three ways to reach new people

Daily posting keeps the page alive. These three bring in new followers.

Collab posts

Gift a set to 6 LA creators (5k–50k followers, real fits, no meme pages). Each one posts a reel as an Instagram Collab with you, so it shows on both pages and their followers see your name. Aim for 2 a week.

About 1,200

Two giveaways

Black Friday (Nov 24–30) and Christmas (Dec 15–21), each for one set. To enter: follow @ethereaaaa.l, tag 2 friends, share it to your story. Co-host each one with one of your collab creators so their followers enter too. Pick the winner live on stories.

About 800

Boost the best reel

Every Monday we put $10 a day behind last week's best reel, set to send people to your profile. That's about $450 in total, and it only goes on with your OK.
